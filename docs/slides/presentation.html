The 3 Problems

PA2 showed three recurring issues for runners: music flow, pace awareness, and in-run interaction.

  • Music flow interruption: 6/7 participants said notifications and coaching break rhythm.
  • Eyes-free pace awareness: 5/7 participants said looking at the screen distracts them.
  • In-run interaction: 5/7 participants reported taps and small targets are unreliable while moving.

Testing Plan

Objectives

Check whether each prototype can be understood quickly and used without looking at the device.

Participants

3 participants, moderated formative testing, no prior knowledge of the prototypes.

Metrics

  1. Cue Interpretation Accuracy (Critical)
  2. Time to recognize pace deviation (seconds)
  3. Number of times runner checked screen (Task Failure)
  4. Facilitator interventions needed
  5. User-reported confidence score (1-5)
Cue Interpretation Accuracy is the key table for 2A and 1C.

Selection + Improvement + Open Questions

Chosen prototype

2A - Haptic Pace Feedback is the selected direction for Hi-Fi.

Evidence and change

It best matches the no-screen goal. The main weakness is cue clarity under motion, so the next version should make the pulse patterns easier to distinguish.

Open questions

  • Which cue felt most immediate?
  • Which prototype would you trust during a real run?
  • What alternative metrics should we track in the hi-fi summative study?
